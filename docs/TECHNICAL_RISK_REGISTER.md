## 1. Risk Scoring Methodology

- **Likelihood:** `High` (Expected to occur in normal operation), `Medium` (Plausible under specific conditions), `Low` (Rare edge case).
- **Impact:** `Critical` (RCE, data loss, legal liability, or core product failure), `High` (Major feature broken or severe UX degradation), `Medium` (Workaround exists), `Low` (Minor annoyance).
- **Severity:** Derived from `Likelihood x Impact` (`Critical`, `High`, `Medium`, `Low`).
